1. Company Structure

Finch MSO is a trade name (dba) of Krieg Holdings I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company. Finch operates as a management services organization ("MSO") providing non-legal operational, administrative, and business support services to independently owned and operated law firms. Finch is not a law firm and does not provide legal services.

All law firms affiliated with or receiving services from Finch maintain full professional independence in the practice of law. Finch does not exercise control over, nor does it interfere with, the professional judgment, client relationships, case strategy, or ethical obligations of any affiliated attorney or law firm.
